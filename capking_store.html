Policy
Returns & Refunds

Return Policy

We want you to love your cap. If you're not satisfied with your purchase, we offer easy returns within 7 days of delivery.

Eligible Items

Items must be unused, in original packaging, with tags attached. We cannot accept returns for hygiene reasons once tags are removed.

How to Return

  1. Contact us at m.abbas.askri@gmail.com or call 0325-6646684
  2. Provide your order number and reason for return
  3. Ship the item back to our Lahore warehouse
  4. Refunds processed within 5-7 business days

Refunds

Refunds are credited to your original payment method. Bank transfers take 3-5 additional business days.

Exchange

We offer size exchanges subject to availability. Same process as returns — just mention your desired size.

Defective Products

If your cap arrives defective or damaged, contact us immediately with photos. We'll replace or refund at no cost.
